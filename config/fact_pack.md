## Company
- JotPsych makes software for behavioral health clinicians. (jotpsych.com)
- Founded/led by Nathan Peereboom (CEO) and Jackson Bierfeldt (CTO). (jotpsych.com/news)
- Raised a $5M seed round led by Base10 Partners, July 2025. (jotpsych.com/news)

## Product
- Ambient AI scribe: turns a recorded session into a clinical note.
- The scribe has drafted more than 2.5 million patient notes. (JotPsych careers page, 2026)
- Reduces administrative/documentation time by roughly 90%. (JotPsych careers page)
- Expanding from the note into the whole practice: billing, audit, prior
  authorization, e-prescribing. (JotPsych careers page)
- JotAudit - AI-driven documentation audit system, launched Feb 2026. (jotpsych.com/blog)
- JotRx - integrated e-prescribing for behavioral health, launched Feb 2026. (jotpsych.com/blog)
- JotMeet - integrated telehealth. (PR Newswire)
- Checks documentation against payer rules and suggests billing codes.
- Works alongside any EHR, or can serve as the EHR.
- HIPAA-compliant, SOC 2. (jotpsych.com)
- Supports multiple input languages; notes are produced in English.
- Mobile app available. Customizable note templates.

## What fits JotPsych, and what does not
FITS
- Time given back: less time on notes, more sessions or an earlier evening.
- Documentation that survives an audit; fewer denials from payer-rule misses.
- Behavioral-health-specific note quality, versus a generic medical scribe.
- Peer evidence: another clinician in the same specialty and state who uses it.
- One product across note -> billing -> audit -> prior auth -> e-prescribe.

DOES NOT FIT - the machine must refuse these
- Any promise about a clinical outcome, a diagnosis, or patient care.
- Any guarantee about reimbursement, payment, or passing an audit.
- Anything referencing a real patient, a session, or note content.
- "HIPAA certified" (no such certification exists) or "FDA approved".
- Volume-and-urgency sales pressure. These are licensed clinicians, not leads.
- Anything that implies the software replaces clinical judgment.
- Competitor bashing by name.
