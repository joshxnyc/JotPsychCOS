## What JotPsych is (current positioning — read this first)
- JotPsych is **the behavioral health EHR that defends you from insurance
  companies**. (jotpsych.com, hero)
- Notes, billing and the whole practice in one system that scans every note and
  claim against **150+ payer rules**, works the denials, and follows every
  dollar from the visit to the bank account. (jotpsych.com)
- It is no longer positioned as a scribe bolted onto someone else's EHR. It is
  the EHR. Writing to a clinician as if it were only a note-taker is wrong.

## The line that matters most to a dormant clinician
- **"Works with any EHR. Or replaces it entirely."** Add JotPsych to the system

- Stated purpose: accurate coding, fewer denials, notes that stand up to an audit.

## What fits, and what does not
FITS
- No migration required; run it beside the current EHR and switch on their timeline.
- Denials, payer rules, coding accuracy, audit defensibility.
- Time back from documentation.
- A practice that just changed shape — new location, new partner, went from solo
  to group — because that is when the software question genuinely reopens.

DOES NOT FIT — the machine must refuse these
- Any promise about a clinical outcome, a diagnosis, or patient care.
- Any guarantee about reimbursement, payment, or passing an audit. JotPsych
  reduces denials and flags risk; it does not guarantee an outcome.
- Anything referencing a real patient, a session, or note content.
- "HIPAA certified" (no such certification exists) or "FDA approved".
- Volume-and-urgency sales pressure. These are licensed clinicians, not leads.
- Anything implying the software replaces clinical judgment.
- Competitor bashing by name.
- **Stating how we knew anything about their practice.** See brand.md.
